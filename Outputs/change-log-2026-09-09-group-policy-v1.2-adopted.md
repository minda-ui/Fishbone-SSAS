# Change log: group document policy v1.2 adopted

**2026-09-09**, following `Outputs/change-log-2026-09-09-group-policy-v1.2-flagged.md`, in which a
second group document-numbering & filing policy notice (v1.2) was found in `Raw/`, independently
verified against the group knowledge base, and raised with the owner for a decision rather than
adopted on this session's own authority. **The owner replied: "adopt v1.2."**

## What changed

`CLAUDE.md` replaced by version 16:

- Section 1's register conventions now reference document-numbering & filing policy **v1.2**
  rather than v1.1, and record its two clarifications:
  1. A task or to-do is not a document and gets no Document Register row or ID.
  2. A KB may migrate its own local back-catalogue into the group register whenever ready, deduping
     on entry, rather than waiting for a central migration.
- Section 6a's introductory line now cites both adoption change-log entries (v1.1's and this one);
  the two exceptions themselves (Smartsheet append, `§7a` inter-KB hand-off) are unchanged, since
  neither v1.2 clarification touches them.
- Open question 12 gained a closing note recording that a second policy file duly appeared the
  same day, was verified and confirmed independently (not adopted by extension of the v1.1
  confirmation), and that the same caution applies to any future version.
- The version banner and footer history line updated to record version 16.

## What did not change

Neither clarification changes this KB's actual behaviour:

- This KB does not raise tasks or to-dos against the group Document Register, so "tasks are not
  documents" was already the de facto practice.
- This KB's own local Document Register (sheet id `2561022001022852`) has never held a row (noted
  at v1.1 adoption), so there is no back-catalogue for it to migrate under the new §11 permission.

No Wiki article changed. No Smartsheet write was made (the group register and Change Requests
sheet are unaffected by this policy update itself).
