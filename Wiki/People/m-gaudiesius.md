## Details

Two signed Empowered Pensions "Authority Form - Information Only Request" forms for his Aviva
employer pension exist: one photographed on 14/10/2021, now on file [^8], and one dated
20/01/2023 in the legacy folder, not filed. The transfer itself was arranged in February 2023
[^10]; the amount is a member benefit figure and is not repeated here.

He signed the [loanback security deed](../Assets/loanback-fishbone-commercial-properties.md) on
both sides of the transaction [^5]. The scheme rules do not invalidate a trustee decision for a
personal interest (rule 6.6) and require the corporate trustee's agreement to lending (rule
6.3.1); see [Scheme rules](../Processes/scheme-rules.md).

The monthly "Pension Contributions" reports for Fishbone Drylining Limited from Galaxy Payroll,
December 2022 to May 2023, in the owner's legacy folder on Drive (id
`1jSFpIOcKb7yANA0hJVtjWb_80rMfvo5c`, outside this knowledge base) label the pension deduction for
both directors "Aviva Salary Sacrifice Pension" `(unverified: seen, not filed; per-member figures
not repeated)`. So the payroll contributions of that period went to Aviva, and any contribution to
the SSAS for him must have come by another route.

Gmail adds that his Aviva transfer was received into the scheme's Metro Bank account by
15/06/2023, with Aviva's closing letter following on 29/06/2023 [^g1] `(email; the amount is a
member benefit figure and is not repeated)`; that he signed the loanback documents through
Signable in April 2025 and the Metro Bank transfer request for the advance on 24/04/2025; and
that on 22/11/2024 a request was lodged for the scheme to reimburse Fishbone Drylining Ltd
1,791.00 for a two-day SSAS workshop under the trustee-expenses declaration. See
[Transfers in](../Finance/transfers-in.md),
[Loanback](../Assets/loanback-fishbone-commercial-properties.md) and
[Empowered Pensions Ltd](../Suppliers/empowered-pensions.md).
