## Details

A signed Empowered Pensions "Authority Form - Information Only Request" dated 20/01/2023 exists in
the legacy folders (both on OneDrive and on Google Drive). It carries the date of birth and
National Insurance number and was **not copied** into this knowledge base; it records an Aviva
employer pension being reviewed for transfer `(unverified: seen in the legacy folder, not on file
here)`.

He signed the [loanback security deed](../Assets/loanback-fishbone-commercial-properties.md) on
both sides of the transaction [^5]. The scheme rules do not invalidate a trustee decision for a
personal interest (rule 6.6) and require the corporate trustee's agreement to lending (rule
6.3.1); see [Scheme rules](../Processes/scheme-rules.md).

The monthly "Pension Contributions" reports for Fishbone Drylining Limited from Galaxy Payroll,
December 2022 to May 2023, in the owner's legacy folder on Drive (id
`1jSFpIOcKb7yANA0hJVtjWb_80rMfvo5c`, outside this knowledge base) label the pension deduction for
both directors "Aviva Salary Sacrifice Pension" `(unverified: seen, not filed; per-member figures
not repeated)`. So the payroll contributions of that period went to Aviva, and any contribution to
the SSAS for him must have come by another route.
