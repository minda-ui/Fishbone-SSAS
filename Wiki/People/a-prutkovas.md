# A Prutkovas (member trustee)

Andrejus Prutkovas: member trustee of the scheme and director of the principal employer. Personal
identifiers in the source documents are deliberately not repeated here.

| Role at the principal employer | Director; present at the establishing meeting; signed the trust deed for the company and as member trustee before a witness, photographed 14/10/2021; "managing director ... from April 2016" per the company's letter of 17/05/2023 | [^2], [^6], [^9] |
| Transfer in | Aviva Designer Pension TK10976612: authority form signed 14/10/2021, transfer arranged March 2023 without advice | [^7], [^8]; see [Transfers in](../Finance/transfers-in.md) |

## Details

His signed Empowered Pensions authority form was photographed on 14/10/2021 and is on file [^7];
a second, dated 20/01/2023, stays in the legacy folder. The Aviva transfer form, the
administrator's letter of 14/03/2023 and his client agreement are on file in one scan [^8]; the
amount is not repeated here, and receipt into the scheme's account is not evidenced. The
employment letter of 17/05/2023, signed by M Gaudiesius, states he is managing director of
Fishbone Drylining Ltd (07948220) from April 2016 and that the company sponsors the SSAS [^9].

He signed the [loanback security deed](../Assets/loanback-fishbone-commercial-properties.md) on
both sides of the transaction [^4]; see the note on rule 6.6 in
[M Gaudiesius](../People/m-gaudiesius.md). The Galaxy Payroll reports described there label his
payroll pension deduction "Aviva Salary Sacrifice Pension" too.

Gmail adds that he submitted the online application to establish and join the Fishbone SSAS on
28/09/2021 [^g1], and that Aviva required him to take a MoneyHelper safeguarding appointment
before releasing his transfer; the administrator chased between 22/06 and 24/07/2023 and a
reference number was sent on 24/07/2023 [^g2]. Completion of that transfer is not evidenced in
the mailbox. See [Transfers in](../Finance/transfers-in.md).
