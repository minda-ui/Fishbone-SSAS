## Details

A signed Empowered Pensions authority form dated 20/01/2023 for an Aviva employer pension exists
in the legacy folders and was **not copied** here (personal identifiers) `(unverified)`. The
legacy folders also hold an Aviva transfer document, a client agreement, an employment letter and
contribution schedules for March to May 2023 in his name, none copied. The file named
`Andrejus client agreemnet.pdf` was rendered to classify it on 2026-09-06: it is a letter from
Empowered Pensions Ltd dated 14/03/2023 confirming that a transfer out in full, in cash, of an
Aviva policy had been arranged at his request without advice `(unverified: seen in the legacy
folder, Drive id 1jSFpIOcKb7yANA0hJVtjWb_80rMfvo5c; amount not repeated here)`. Whether the
transfer reached the scheme is not evidenced.

He signed the [loanback security deed](../Assets/loanback-fishbone-commercial-properties.md) on
both sides of the transaction [^4]; see the note on rule 6.6 in
[M Gaudiesius](../People/m-gaudiesius.md). The Galaxy Payroll reports described there label his
payroll pension deduction "Aviva Salary Sacrifice Pension" too.
