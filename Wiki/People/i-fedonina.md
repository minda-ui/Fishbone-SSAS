documents on file as a party to the Legal Mortgage of 29 April 2025. Personal identifiers and
addresses in the source documents are deliberately not repeated here.

## Details

She is not named in the trust deed or the board resolution of 2021, which appoint two member
trustees, nor in the scheme's TPR registration of August 2022, which lists two individual
trustees and two active members. See
[Establishment 2021](../Processes/scheme-establishment-2021.md) and
[TPR scheme registration 2022](../Finance/tpr-scheme-registration-2022.md). Under the scheme
rules the member trustees may appoint an additional trustee by deed (rule 2.1.1) and the trustees
may admit as a member an employee or director of a participating employer (rule 14.1); see
[Scheme rules](../Processes/scheme-rules.md).

The owner's legacy folder on Drive (folder `SSAS`, id `1jSFpIOcKb7yANA0hJVtjWb_80rMfvo5c`, outside
this knowledge base) holds an Empowered Pensions "Small Self-Administered Scheme 'Additional
Member' form" for the Fishbone SSAS in her name, in two versions dated December 2023 and January
2024, with the role "Member & Trustee" `(unverified: seen in the legacy folder, not filed here
because it carries personal identifiers)`. The same folder holds payslips showing her as an
employee of Fishbone Properties Ltd, and a draft letter of February 2025 from that company's
director to Nest Pension stating that her pension contributions are made to the Fishbone SSAS
`(unverified, same reason)`.
