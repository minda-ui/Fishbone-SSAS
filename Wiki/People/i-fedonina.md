# I Fedonina (member trustee)

Irina Fedonina: a third individual trustee of the scheme, appearing for the first time in the
documents on file as a party to the Legal Mortgage of 29 April 2025. Personal identifiers and
addresses in the source documents are deliberately not repeated here.

## Details

The sequence on file: application to join as member and trustee signed 05/01/2024 [^2]; added
to the Metro Bank mandate as the third member and trustee in May 2024 [^3]; Aviva and Nest
transfers arranged from October 2024 [^5]; party to and signatory of the loanback security in
April 2025 [^1]. She is not named in the trust deed or the board resolution of 2021, which
appoint two member trustees, nor in the scheme's TPR registration of August 2022, which lists two
individual trustees and two active members. See
[Establishment 2021](../Processes/scheme-establishment-2021.md) and
[TPR scheme registration 2022](../Finance/tpr-scheme-registration-2022.md). Under the scheme
rules the member trustees may appoint an additional trustee by deed (rule 2.1.1) and the trustees
may admit as a member an employee or director of a participating employer (rule 14.1); see
[Scheme rules](../Processes/scheme-rules.md).

Her employer, [Fishbone Properties Ltd](../Employers/fishbone-properties-ltd.md), is not on file
as a participating employer; its letters say it pays her contributions to the SSAS, and its
employment letter says Fishbone Drylining Ltd is the sponsoring company [^4]. See
[Contributions](../Finance/contributions.md). Payslips and identity documents in the legacy
folder were not filed.

## Open questions

- The deed of appointment as trustee (the bank asked for one in May 2024 and the administrator
  says it sent the deeds off on 29/05/2024 [^6], so it exists at the administrator) and the
  trustees' resolution admitting her as a member.
- Whether Fishbone Properties Ltd, her apparent employer, was admitted as a participating
  employer by deed under rule 12.1; without that, the basis of her membership is rule 14.2 or
  unclear. See [Fishbone Construction Ltd](../Employers/fishbone-construction-ltd.md).
- Whether TPR (scheme return) and HMRC were told of the change of trustees and members.
- What has been contributed for her, and by whom.
