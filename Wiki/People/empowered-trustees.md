# Empowered Trustees Ltd (corporate trustee)

The independent corporate trustee appointed alongside the two member trustees.

## Details

A sister company of the scheme administrator,
[Empowered Pensions Ltd](../Suppliers/empowered-pensions.md), on the evidence of the names; the
relationship between the two is not documented on file `(unverified)`. The execution block for
the corporate trustee on page 4 of the trust deed copy is blank.

Under the [scheme rules](../Processes/scheme-rules.md), rule 6.3, the corporate trustee's written
agreement is required before the trustees exercise their investment and lending powers, appoint
or remove the administrator, amend the rules or wind up. Its execution of the
[loanback security deed](../Assets/loanback-fishbone-commercial-properties.md) on 29/04/2025,
through its director Lars Ian Singleton, is the first evidence on file of it acting [^3] p.43.
