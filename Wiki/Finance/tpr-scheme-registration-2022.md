## Details

The PSR number 12018880 is the number the employer later used as the scheme's EPSR on its
automatic enrolment re-declaration; see
[TPR re-declaration 2023](../Finance/tpr-re-declaration-2023.md). The HMRC approval number is
the PSTR in [HMRC registration](../Finance/hmrc-registration.md).

The commencement date of 28/10/2021 is three days before the member trustees signed the Metro
Bank account opening request and is the earliest date on file for the scheme's existence; the
executed trust deed, which is not on file, is presumably dated on or about it `(unverified)`. See
[Establishment 2021](../Processes/scheme-establishment-2021.md).

The company number given here for the employer, 07948220, agrees with the trust deed and the
board minutes and disagrees with the 07948020 typed on the November 2023 re-declaration, so the
re-declaration is now the odd one out. See
[Fishbone Construction Ltd](../Employers/fishbone-construction-ltd.md).

The registration shows two trustees and two members. By 29/04/2025 there were three individual
trustees; see [I Fedonina](../People/i-fedonina.md). Whether TPR was told, through a scheme
return or an Exchange update, is not evidenced.
