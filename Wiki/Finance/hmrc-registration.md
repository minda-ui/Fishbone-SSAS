# HMRC registration (PSTR 20005255RF)

The scheme's registration with HMRC for tax relief and exemptions, and the standing obligations
the registration letter sets out.

## Details

Tax relief and exemptions run from the registration date. The letter says the PSTR must be used in
all future communications with HMRC, that a change to the scheme structure or to investment
regulation must be reported on an Event Report, and that transfers out must be recognised
transfers under section 169 Finance Act 2004 or they are unauthorised member payments carrying a
scheme sanction charge of up to 40 percent [^1] p.1. HMRC may de-register the scheme if the
conditions in section 158 Finance Act 2004 apply, with a de-registration charge of 40 percent of
the scheme's assets, and the administrator must remain a fit and proper person under section
270(3) [^1] p.2.

For a scheme that lends to a sponsoring employer, the "investment-regulated" declaration matters:
the loanback to Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd must meet the authorised employer loan
conditions (security, interest rate, term, amount not exceeding 50 percent of scheme assets,
equal instalments). None of that has been checked in this knowledge base yet, and the loanback
agreement is not on file.
