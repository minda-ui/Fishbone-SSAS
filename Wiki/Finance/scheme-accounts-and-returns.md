# Scheme accounts and returns (year end 5 April)

What is known about the scheme's annual accounts and its returns to HMRC and The Pensions
Regulator. **From 2026-09-07 the administrator's own accounts for the year to 5 April 2025 are on
file**: a Statement of Income and Expenditure, a Balance Sheet, and the supporting scheme
valuation and transaction list. Earlier and later years are still not on file; see Open questions.

## Details

The administrator's covering email treats the accounts as the basis of a submission to HMRC,
which for a registered scheme means the Pension Scheme Return, so the 2021-22 return was
presumably made after the owner's approval `(unverified)`. The same email carries the scheme's
Metro Bank details; see [Metro Bank](../Suppliers/metro-bank.md). The loanback of April 2025
falls in the year to 05/04/2026, whose accounts should show the loan receivable and the
repayments; see [Loanback](../Assets/loanback-fishbone-commercial-properties.md). HMRC's standing
obligations are in [HMRC registration](../Finance/hmrc-registration.md).

**The year to 5 April 2025 is the closest verified snapshot of the scheme's value before the
loanback.** The Statement of Account [^2] and the fuller Scheme Valuation bundle [^3] agree on
83,701.24: the scheme held nothing but a Metro Bank deposit at that date, the loan not yet drawn.
This is the figure used for the loanback's 50 percent test; see
[Loanback](../Assets/loanback-fishbone-commercial-properties.md). The "other expenditure" of
1,791.00 on 02/12/2024, per the transaction list [^3] p.6, is the reimbursement requested by
Fishbone Drylining Ltd in November 2024; see
[Fishbone Construction Ltd](../Employers/fishbone-construction-ltd.md). The comparative column
also shows the scheme's assets going from 496 to 86,062 over the year to 05/04/2024, the year the
first two members' Aviva transfers completed, consistent with (but not a substitute for)
[Transfers in](../Finance/transfers-in.md).

The Scheme Valuation bundle [^3] also carries member-level detail (dates of birth, National
Insurance numbers, addresses, spouse names, individual fund values): `sensitive`, not quoted here;
see the bundle's own front matter for what it contains.
