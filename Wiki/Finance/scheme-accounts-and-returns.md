## Details

The administrator's covering email treats the accounts as the basis of a submission to HMRC,
which for a registered scheme means the Pension Scheme Return, so the 2021-22 return was
presumably made after the owner's approval `(unverified)`. The same email carries the scheme's
Metro Bank details; see [Metro Bank](../Suppliers/metro-bank.md). The loanback of April 2025
falls in the year to 05/04/2026, whose accounts should show the loan receivable and the
repayments; see [Loanback](../Assets/loanback-fishbone-commercial-properties.md). HMRC's standing
obligations are in [HMRC registration](../Finance/hmrc-registration.md).
