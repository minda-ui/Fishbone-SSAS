# Contributions to the scheme

What is known about money paid into the scheme by employers and members, as distinct from
[transfers in](../Finance/transfers-in.md). The honest summary is that **no document on file
shows a contribution reaching the scheme**; the documents show payroll deductions going to
Aviva, and two employer letters asserting that a third employer pays into the SSAS. Per-employee
figures in the payroll reports are not repeated here.

## Key facts

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Payroll pension reports on file | six monthly "Pension Contributions" reports from Galaxy Payroll for Fishbone Drylining Limited, pay dates 02/12/2022, 04/01/2023, 01/02/2023, 03/03/2023, 06/04/2023, 03/05/2023 | [^1] [^2] [^3] [^4] [^5] [^6] |
| Pension named on those reports | "Aviva Salary Sacrifice Pension" for all three employees listed, the two directors and one other | [^1] p.1 |
| Contributions to the SSAS shown on those reports | none | [^1] [^2] [^3] [^4] [^5] [^6] |
| Employer letter claiming SSAS contributions | Fishbone Properties Ltd (09687012) to Aviva, 2025: its employee's "pension contributions made to Fishbone SSAS ... including employer-matching contributions" are on her payslips | [^7] |

## Details

The Galaxy Payroll reports are the principal employer's monthly pension deduction schedules. Each
lists the three employees with pensionable pay and employee and employer percentages under the
heading "Aviva Salary Sacrifice Pension" [^1] [^2] [^3] [^4] [^5] [^6]. Nothing on them refers to the SSAS. So in
that period the principal employer's automatic enrolment contributions went to Aviva, which
matches the TPR re-declaration listing both Aviva and the SSAS as schemes used; see
[TPR re-declaration 2023](../Finance/tpr-re-declaration-2023.md). Any contribution to the SSAS
for the two directors in that period must have been paid outside payroll `(unverified)`.

For the third member, Fishbone Properties Ltd wrote to Aviva in 2025, and in identical draft to
Nest in February 2025 `(legacy folder, not filed)`, saying her contributions "made on my behalf
by Fishbone Properties, including employer-matching contributions" go to the Fishbone SSAS and
can be seen on her payslips [^7]. Her payslips in the legacy folder show a "Pension" deduction
line `(seen, not filed; figures not repeated)`. The Fishbone Properties Ltd knowledge base
records weekly 35.00 payments from that company's Starling account to a payee "Metro SSAS
Account" `(unverified here)`. See [Fishbone Properties Ltd](../Employers/fishbone-properties-ltd.md).

What is missing is the receiving side: the scheme's [Metro Bank](../Suppliers/metro-bank.md)
statements, and any schedule of contributions or payment schedule that the administrator holds.

The administrator's position on how contributions are made is in its email of 19/01/2023: "there
is no paperwork required ... it would just be transferring your chosen amount into the Metro
Bank account" [^8]. The same email sent the bank details, which the owner said he did not have
at that date, so no contribution by the principal employer or its directors is likely before
January 2023 `(inference)`.

## Open questions

- Metro Bank statements from opening to date, to see what contributions arrived, from which
  employer, and for whom.
- Whether the principal employer has ever contributed to the SSAS, as opposed to Aviva.
- The basis on which Fishbone Properties Ltd contributes (a deed of participation under rule 12.1
  is not on file).
- Whether member contributions were claimed for tax relief correctly (relief at source does not
  apply to an occupational scheme; employer and salary-sacrifice contributions need no claim).
