# Loanback to Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd (secured on 145 High Street East, Wallsend)

The scheme's one known investment: a loan of 41,500 to Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd, a
group company, secured by a first legal mortgage over 145 High Street East, Wallsend. The
security deed, the solicitors' completion statement and their receipted invoice are on file. **The
loan agreement itself, dated 16 April 2025, is not**, so the rate, term and repayment schedule
below still rest on the group's Loans wiki and are `(unverified)`.

Asset Register id: none yet. When issued it will be `FSS 0001` with `Asset class` "Loanback
receivable", `Loan type` "SSAS loanback", `Counterparty` Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd.

## Details

**Timeline from the documents.** The solicitors' invoice has a tax point of 16/04/2025 [^3]; the
loan agreement is dated 16 April 2025 [^1] p.6; the mortgage deed is dated 29 April 2025 [^1]
cover; the completion statement is undated but carries the same matter reference [^2]. So the
money moved on or about 29/04/2025, which fits the borrower's monthly payment date of about the
29th recorded in the sister knowledge base and the final payment date of 28/04/2030 in the Loans
wiki.

**What the completion statement shows.** From the 41,500 loan the solicitors deducted their fee
of 950 plus VAT, a bank transfer fee, company, winding-up, Land Registry and land charges
searches, anti-money-laundering identity checks for three names, office copy entries, a Companies
House filing fee of 15.00 and a Land Registration fee of 45.00, total 1,352.20, and advanced
40,147.80 to the borrower [^2]. The receipted invoice covers 1,244.20 of that; the balance of
108.00 is the third identity check plus the registration and search fees that appear only on the
completion statement [^2] [^3]. The borrower therefore received 40,147.80 net but owes the full
41,500 `(inference from the statement, not stated in it)`.

**Charge registration.** The Companies House filing fee and the Land Registration fee were paid
[^2], which implies an application to register the charge against the borrower at Companies House
and the restriction at the Land Registry. **Neither registration is evidenced** on file
`(unverified)`; see Open questions.

**The deed.** The trustees are the Lender. The Secured Liabilities are everything owed under the
Loan Agreement and any other loan agreement between the same parties [^1] p.6. Events of default
include non-payment, breach, insolvency and a value-of-assets test [^1] pp.4-5. The Lender's
maximum exposure is tied to the scheme's own value, not to a fixed sum [^1] p.6. Clauses 6.11 to
29 (representations, general and property covenants, powers of the Lender, enforcement,
receivers, costs, release, notices; PDF pages 14 to 39) were not summarised in this session. The
plan referred to in Schedule 1 is not part of the scanned copy.

**Both sides of the table.** The two directors who signed for the borrower are two of the four
trustees who signed for the lender [^1] pp.40, 42, 44. The scheme rules allow a trustee to act
despite a personal interest (rule 6.6) and require the corporate trustee's written agreement to
exercise the investment and lending powers (rule 6.3.1); Empowered Trustees Ltd executed the deed
[^1] p.43. See [Scheme rules](../Processes/scheme-rules.md).

## Open questions

- The **loan agreement dated 16 April 2025**: principal, rate, term, repayment schedule, default
  interest. Dollman & Pritchard (ref AJA/FIS53.1) or Empowered Pensions Ltd should hold it.
- Evidence that the charge was registered at Companies House against company 13687238 (form
  MR01, within 21 days of 29/04/2025) and that the Form P restriction was entered on title TY59507.
- The valuation of 145 High Street East relied on for the loan, and whether the repairs it
  assumed are complete.
- The scheme's value at 16/04/2025, to test the 50 percent limit.
- The repayment record from the scheme's Metro Bank statements (none on file).
- Whether the loan was reported to HMRC where an Event Report requires it.
