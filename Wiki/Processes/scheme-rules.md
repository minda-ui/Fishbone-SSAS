# Scheme rules (Empowered Pension Ltd SSAS Scheme Rules)

The rules that the trust deed says govern the scheme. The copy on file is the administrator's
standard "SSAS Scheme Rules" document, 16 pages, naming the Fishbone SSAS in rule 1.1, with
signature blocks for the two member trustees and Empowered Trustees Ltd on page 16 that are
**blank and undated**. A photograph taken on 14/10/2021 shows that page signed by both member
trustees, the corporate trustee's line and all three dates blank [^2]. A fully executed copy is
not on file.

## Key facts

| Rule | What it says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Governance | the SSAS is governed by the Rules; the Trustees hold the Fund on irrevocable trusts and may determine who is a Beneficiary and any question of doubt | [^1] p.3 |
| 2 Trustees | member trustees may by deed appoint or remove any trustee; a trustee may retire by written notice; no minimum number of trustees, maximum eleven | [^1] p.3 |
| 3 Investment | full powers of investment, including land, collectives, derivatives and bank accounts; may lend to and borrow from any person on such security and terms as they see fit; may exercise the powers even where a trustee or connected person has an interest | [^1] pp.3-4 |
| 4 Powers | may delegate; may operate a bank account; a corporate trustee may operate an account alone on behalf of the trustees | [^1] p.4 |
| 6 Proceedings | decisions by written resolution or meeting; only with the unanimous agreement of the member trustees; the **written agreement of any corporate trustee is required** to exercise the rule 3 powers, to appoint or remove the scheme administrator, to amend the rules, to exercise the rule 18.4 powers or to resolve to wind up; a decision is not invalidated by a trustee's personal interest | [^1] pp.4-5 |
| 7 Duties | keep books and records; arrange a statement of accounts, audited if the law requires | [^1] p.5 |
| 8 Liability | trustees not liable except for their own fraud, dishonesty, wilful neglect or default; member trustees indemnified out of the Fund or by the employers; may take out trustee liability insurance | [^1] p.6 |
| 10 Costs | all costs of the SSAS are met out of the Fund unless met by another person | [^1] p.6 |
| 11 Administrator | a person meeting section 270(2) Finance Act 2004 must be appointed at all times | [^1] p.6 |
| 12 Employers | an employer is admitted as a Participating Employer **by a deed** in which it covenants to comply with the Rules; the Principal Employer may be substituted by deed | [^1] p.7 |
| 13 Amendment | by deed, with retrospective, immediate or future effect | [^1] p.7 |
| 14 Membership | the trustees may admit any employee or director of a participating employer, and any other person whose admission is consistent with occupational and registered scheme status | [^1] p.7 |
| 16 Contributions | any person may contribute with the trustees' consent; allocated to Individual Funds as directed, otherwise to the General Fund | [^1] pp.7-8 |
| 18 to 24 Benefits | drawdown, lump sums, death benefits, transfers in and out, payment of benefit | [^1] pp.8-12 |
| 32 Winding-up | on trustee resolution or on the 124th anniversary of establishment | [^1] p.13 |
| 34 Definitions | "Trust Deed" means the deed to which the Rules are attached; "SSAS" is as defined in the Trust Deed; "Member Trustee" is a person so defined in the Trust Deed or appointed under rule 2 | [^1] pp.14-16 |

## Details

The rules are generic to the administrator's SSAS product; only rule 1.1 names this scheme. Three
provisions matter most for what the scheme has actually done:

- **Lending to a group company** is within rule 3.2.1, and rule 3.3.3 allows it even where a
  trustee has an interest, but rule 6.3.1 requires the corporate trustee's written agreement.
  Empowered Trustees Ltd executed the Legal Mortgage of 29/04/2025, which is consistent with that
  agreement having been given; see
  [Loanback to Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd](../Assets/loanback-fishbone-commercial-properties.md).
- **A new trustee** is appointed by deed under rule 2.1.1, and **a new member** under rule 14
  must ordinarily be an employee or director of a participating employer, which under rule 12.1
  is admitted by deed. The appointment of [I Fedonina](../People/i-fedonina.md) and the status of
  Fishbone Properties Ltd should each be evidenced by such a deed.
- **Accounts** must be kept and, where the law requires, audited (rule 7.2); no accounts are on
  file.

The rules refer to the Trust Deed for the definition of the SSAS and of the first member
trustees, so the executed deed and rules belong together; see
[Establishment 2021](../Processes/scheme-establishment-2021.md).

## Open questions

- Deeds of appointment of trustees and of participation of employers made under rules 2 and 12.
