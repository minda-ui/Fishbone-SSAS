# Establishment of the Fishbone SSAS, 2021

How the scheme came into being, from the documents on file: a board resolution of the principal
employer, a trust deed appointing two member trustees and a corporate trustee, a bank account
opening request in October 2021, and HMRC registration in December 2021.

## Details

The board minutes record a meeting of the directors of Fishbone Drylining Ltd at 6 Beverley Place,
Wallsend, with both directors present, which resolved to establish an occupational pension scheme
with the company as Scheme Establisher and Principal Employer, to appoint the two directors as
initial member trustees, to appoint Empowered Pensions Ltd as scheme administrator and Empowered
Trustees Ltd as independent corporate trustee [^2]. **The copy on file is undated and unsigned**:
the date line reads `[__/__/____]` and the four signature lines are blank.

The trust deed establishes the scheme "with effect on and from the date of this deed", says the
scheme is governed by "the rules attached to this deed", appoints the member trustees and the
corporate trustee as first trustees, records the principal employer's intention to apply to HMRC
for registration under Part 4 of the Finance Act 2004, and requires the first trustees to appoint
a scheme administrator under section 270(2) of that Act [^1] p.2. **The copy on file is also
undated and unsigned** (four pages; the execution blocks on pages 3 and 4 are blank) and **the
rules are not attached** to it. The rules are now on file as a separate, equally unsigned copy
[^5]; see [Scheme rules](../Processes/scheme-rules.md).

The Metro Bank "Pension Scheme Account Opening Request" was signed by both member trustees on
31/10/2021; only page 8 (the declaration and signatures) is on file [^4]. See
[Metro Bank](../Suppliers/metro-bank.md).

HMRC registered the scheme on 03/12/2021 as an occupational and investment-regulated pension
scheme and issued its notification to Empowered Pensions Ltd on 06/12/2021 [^3]. See
[HMRC registration](../Finance/hmrc-registration.md).

Sequence, from the dates that are on documents: commencement 28/10/2021 as declared to TPR [^6],
bank account request 31/10/2021, HMRC registration 03/12/2021, notification 06/12/2021, TPR
registration submitted 04/08/2022 [^6]. The resolution and the deed carry no date; the executed
deed is presumably dated on or about the commencement date `(unverified)`. See
[TPR scheme registration 2022](../Finance/tpr-scheme-registration-2022.md).
