# Establishment of the Fishbone SSAS, 2021

How the scheme came into being, from the documents on file: a board resolution of the principal
employer, a trust deed appointing two member trustees and a corporate trustee, a bank account
opening request in October 2021, and HMRC registration in December 2021.

## Details

The board minutes record a meeting of the directors of Fishbone Drylining Ltd at 6 Beverley Place,
Wallsend, with both directors present, which resolved to establish an occupational pension scheme
with the company as Scheme Establisher and Principal Employer, to appoint the two directors as
initial member trustees, to appoint Empowered Pensions Ltd as scheme administrator and Empowered
Trustees Ltd as independent corporate trustee [^2]. **The PDF copy on file is undated and unsigned**:
the date line reads `[__/__/____]` and the four signature lines are blank. A photograph taken
on 14/10/2021 shows the same page signed by both directors, the date still blank [^8].

The trust deed establishes the scheme "with effect on and from the date of this deed", says the
scheme is governed by "the rules attached to this deed", appoints the member trustees and the
corporate trustee as first trustees, records the principal employer's intention to apply to HMRC
for registration under Part 4 of the Finance Act 2004, and requires the first trustees to appoint
a scheme administrator under section 270(2) of that Act [^1] p.2. **The PDF copy on file is also
undated and unsigned** (four pages; the execution blocks on pages 3 and 4 are blank) and **the
rules are not attached** to it. A photograph taken the same morning shows page 3 executed by
Fishbone Drylining Ltd through both directors and signed as a deed by each member trustee, each
witnessed, with the printed words "but not delivered until dated" [^7]; page 4, the corporate
trustee's execution, is not among the photographs. The rules' signature page and the
administration agreement's signature page were photographed signed by both member trustees, with
the corporate trustee's and the administrator's lines blank [^9] [^10]. The rules are now on file as a separate, equally unsigned copy
[^5]; see [Scheme rules](../Processes/scheme-rules.md).

The Metro Bank "Pension Scheme Account Opening Request" was signed by both member trustees on
31/10/2021; only page 8 (the declaration and signatures) is on file [^4]. See
[Metro Bank](../Suppliers/metro-bank.md).

HMRC registered the scheme on 03/12/2021 as an occupational and investment-regulated pension
scheme and issued its notification to Empowered Pensions Ltd on 06/12/2021 [^3]. See
[HMRC registration](../Finance/hmrc-registration.md).

Gmail supplies the start of the sequence: the application to join the Fishbone SSAS was
submitted online by A Prutkovas on 28/09/2021 (scheme reference EP/EApp/74) [^11], and on
30/09/2021 the administrator sent the trust deed, scheme rules, administration agreement, board
minutes and authority forms, asking for the deed's page 3, the rules' page 16, the agreement's
page 20, the minutes and the authority forms to be returned signed but **undated**, so that the
corporate trustee's director could sign and date them [^12]. That is the set photographed on
14/10/2021 and is why no copy on file carries a date: the dated originals are with the
administrator `(email; attachments not on file)`.

Sequence, from the dates that are on documents: application 28/09/2021 [^11], pack sent
30/09/2021 [^12], governing documents signed by the company and
the member trustees on or before 14/10/2021 (photograph timestamps) [^7] to [^10], commencement
28/10/2021 as declared to TPR [^6], bank account request 31/10/2021, HMRC registration 03/12/2021, notification 06/12/2021, TPR
registration submitted 04/08/2022 [^6]. The resolution and the deed carry no date in any copy; the deed
was presumably dated on delivery, on or about the commencement date, after the corporate trustee
signed `(unverified)`. See
[TPR scheme registration 2022](../Finance/tpr-scheme-registration-2022.md).
