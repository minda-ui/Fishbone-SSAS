## Details

HMRC's notification of registration is addressed to Empowered Pensions Ltd, which makes it the
scheme administrator for HMRC purposes, with the responsibilities set out on page 2 of that letter
(fit and proper person, section 270(3) Finance Act 2004) [^2].

**How the scheme was set up, from Gmail.** A Prutkovas submitted the online application to join
the Fishbone SSAS on 28/09/2021 (Zoho form, scheme reference EP/EApp/74) [^9]. On 30/09/2021
Sacha Bullock sent the establishment pack (trust deed, scheme rules, administration agreement,
board minutes, authority forms) with the instruction to return the deed's page 3, the rules'
page 16, the agreement's page 20, the minutes and the letters of authority signed but **undated**,
for Lars Singleton to sign and date [^10]; that is exactly the set photographed on 14/10/2021 and
explains why every copy on file is undated. The Metro Bank account was applied for by the
administrator between October and December 2021 `(email subjects)`. On 19/01/2023 the
administrator told the owner that contributions need no paperwork, "just transferring your chosen
amount into the Metro Bank account", sent the bank details password-protected, and sent the
**annual accounts for 06/04/2021 to 05/04/2022** for approval before submission to HMRC [^11];
see [Scheme accounts and returns](../Finance/scheme-accounts-and-returns.md).

The administration agreement and its fee schedule are now on file. The agreement itself could
not be read: the Drive connector's download fails at its 8.8 MB size and it has no text layer, so
its terms (services, term, notice, what the administrator does about accounts, returns and Event
Reports) remain unknown until a person reads it [^6]. The fee schedule, two pages headed
"Schedule 1 - Scheme fees for Services" with the footer "Scheme Administration Agreement, page 21
of 33, 2018-04-v1.0", was read as page images [^4]. Fees relevant to this scheme, all as printed
in 2018 and therefore `(unverified)` as current rates:

The notes say all fees other than establishment are chargeable to the scheme bank account, and
that for lending the administrator provides the appointed solicitor with the signed loan documents
within ten working days using the "Empowered Pensions loan Typeform" [^4] p.22. The Additional
Member form in the legacy folder quotes a different figure for an additional member (750 plus VAT
as the "total initial fee") `(unverified: form text, not on file)`; which applied to the 2024
admission is unknown.

The scheme's TPR registration was submitted by Empowered Pensions Ltd on 04/08/2022 and gives the
administrator's address as the scheme address and Mrs Sacha Bullock as the contact [^5]. See
[TPR scheme registration 2022](../Finance/tpr-scheme-registration-2022.md). The administrator
also supplied the [scheme rules](../Processes/scheme-rules.md) and sits behind the corporate
trustee, [Empowered Trustees Ltd](../People/empowered-trustees.md), which executed the
[loanback security](../Assets/loanback-fishbone-commercial-properties.md) in April 2025.
