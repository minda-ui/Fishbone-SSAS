## Details

HMRC's notification of registration is addressed to Empowered Pensions Ltd, which makes it the
scheme administrator for HMRC purposes, with the responsibilities set out on page 2 of that letter
(fit and proper person, section 270(3) Finance Act 2004) [^2].

Two documents about the relationship exist in the OneDrive source folder but **could not be
copied** into this knowledge base: `Admistration Agreement.pdf` (8.8 MB) and
`SASS price list.pdf` (1.2 MB). They are the obvious next Raw items.
