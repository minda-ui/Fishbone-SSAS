# Fishbone Properties Ltd (employer of the third member; participation not evidenced)

A group company that employs the scheme's third member and says it pays her pension
contributions to the SSAS. Whether it has been admitted as a participating employer under the
scheme rules is not evidenced by anything on file.

## Details

The employment letter of 04/11/2024 also states that "Fishbone Drylining Ltd is sponsoring
company of Fishbone SSAS" [^1], and the scheme's TPR registration of 2022 lists Fishbone Drylining
Ltd as the only employer; see
[Fishbone Construction Ltd](../Employers/fishbone-construction-ltd.md) and
[TPR scheme registration 2022](../Finance/tpr-scheme-registration-2022.md). Under rule 12.1 of
the [scheme rules](../Processes/scheme-rules.md) an employer is admitted as a participating
employer by a deed in which it covenants to comply with the rules, and under rule 14.1 a member
is ordinarily an employee or director of a participating employer. So either a deed of
participation exists and is not on file, or the third member was admitted under rule 14.2, or
the admission is irregular. The Gmail record narrows this: the administrator raised a Deed of
Adherence for this company in April 2025, after the member had joined in January 2024 and been
added to the bank mandate in May 2024, which suggests the company was not adhered before then
[^4] [^5]. The same administrator sent a Deed of Adherence for Fishbone Commercial Properties
Ltd, the loanback borrower, on 09/04/2025 and then said it was already adhered; see
[Loanback](../Assets/loanback-fishbone-commercial-properties.md). The ceding providers' questionnaires for her transfers relied on her
employment by this company for the statutory employment link [^3].

This company's own knowledge base (`Fishbone Properties Ltd`) holds its Starling bank statements,
which the sister session recorded as showing weekly 35.00 payments to "Metro SSAS Account"
`(unverified here)`. Link there rather than copy; see
[Contributions](../Finance/contributions.md).
